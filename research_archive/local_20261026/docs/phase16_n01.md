## Later ensemble plan, not authorized for execution here

If N01 contributes independent validation gain and the five-model latency is viable, a three-seed N01 set is the lower-complexity next comparison: train two additional fresh seeds under a fixed schedule, with no use of Phase 15 Holdout. The present preflight suggests roughly 21 minutes per training epoch before validation; at 10–15 epochs per seed, two more seeds would cost about 7–11 GPU hours, with actual cost dependent on stopping. Each additional N01 checkpoint is expected to add roughly 80 MB and one image forward; actual bytes and latency require measurement. E03 plus three N01 variants might fit 500 MB, but it must be checked from saved checkpoint files. A patient-level five-fold set would require roughly five fresh fits per backbone and substantially greater training and inference cost; fixed folds, per-fold checkpoint selection and ensemble weights must be declared before any fold training. Neither route may reuse the Phase 15 Holdout for choice or tuning.
