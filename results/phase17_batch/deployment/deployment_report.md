# Deployment hard constraints

Weights <=500,000,000 bytes AND official full-pipeline latency <=100ms. 80ms is an internal safety target, not an official cutoff. Only PASS is deployable; all other states are excluded. RTX4090/RTX4060 timings are research evidence, not target-hardware compliance. FP16/quantization/distillation require fresh accuracy and timing for exact artifacts.
